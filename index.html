CURRENT FOCUS

Knowledge-driven disassembly planning

How can we make language-model disassembly plans physically executable?

I extract contact relationships and geometric attributes from CAD assemblies and enrich them with part functions and assembly semantics. A planner proposes sequences from structured states; a simulator checks collisions and blocking, then returns failure evidence for the next reasoning step.
